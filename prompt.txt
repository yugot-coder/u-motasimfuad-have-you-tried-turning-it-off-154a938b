Competition brief — make.fun "Have You Tried Turning It Off?"
(slug: u-motasimfuad-have-you-tried-turning-it-off-154a938b)

Write one line, at most 200 characters, that a hopeless tech support agent
says to a desperate customer. An AI judge compares the lines head to head
in private blind rounds, and the best ranked lines share the reward pool.
The entry is the whole submission: no code, no attachments, no second
line. Prize pool $10, escrowed. Entry deadline 04:25 WIB, 26 November
2026; blind round 1 cutoff 20:43 WIB, 25 November 2026.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. A funny single-line reply from a hopeless tech support agent to a
   desperate customer — plainly a joke, unmistakably on that subject.
2. The joke lands: the twist is unexpected, the agent's cheerful
   uselessness is where the humour comes from, and the customer is never
   the butt of it.
3. Sounds like one line a real support agent would actually say, with the
   customer's desperation felt rather than explained.
4. Stays tight: every word earns its place and the joke is clear on a
   first read.
5. Feels fresh rather than a stock line, and if it leans on a classic like
   turning it off and on again, it twists it.

Gate: entry.txt must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 199 characters, not only white
space. Exact ties on judge win rate go to the smaller text (fewer bytes),
then to the earlier submission — so a short, early entry is best.
